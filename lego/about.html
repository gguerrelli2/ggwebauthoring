Our Website Through Time

2020

LEGO released their new look for adult LEGO sets for the first time with the "Fairground Collection Haunted House" set. The new look labelled the sets as "18+" for the first time, and features a simplistic black box art. This is when we felt that LEGO truly embraced the adult market as target segment for the first time, which piqued our interest because of their shift in market strategy. Research for the Brick Corner went underway.

2021

The “Super Mario 64 ? Block” set was released. The team became interested in the massive success of the set as the first 18+ LEGO Super Mario set. Our Site Manager decided to produce an in-depth review on the set, as it was a great representative for the amount of detail and nostalgia that LEGO aimed to include in an 18+ set. The team took their first voyage to the LEGO Store in Rockefeller Center, New York City. The Reviews and Visual Research Gallery pages were formulated.

2022

Adobe Dreamweaver came out of its Beta stage and became the platform on which the Brick Corner is developed. New forms of code and design were unlocked with this new setup. Brick Corner advances visually.

2023

With Super Mario, Marvel, and Star Wars consistently receiving 18+ and -18 sets, sales data was gathered by our Research Specialist. New data was brought in to analyze. Our team compared the sales numbers of sets from each of these series as added analysis on what market LEGO is the most successful in.

2024

The Super Mario 64 ? Block LEGO set was retired. The set was listed for 156% of its retail price on Amazon. Despite this, it has many monthly purchasers, even to this day. Brick Corner recognized the integrity of retired sets and valued this new factor in our research moving forward.

2025

The first LEGO factory since Brick Corner was created opened in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam! The Brick Corner expanded its research to a global scale, taking into account what resources LEGO has for each region of the world. The Map page was born.

2026

The Map page was updated, featuring the latest Factory and Regional Distribution Center pair in Virginia, USA. New facts for each LEGO location were uncovered by the Research Team. The Visual Essay was written to pull together all the data Brick Corner has gathered thus far. Our scope of research continues to grow and grow!
