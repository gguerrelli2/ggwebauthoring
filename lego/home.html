What's here?

Entrepreneurship Corner provides an in-depth analysis on LEGO from a business and marketing standpoint. You can find all types of research on LEGO's marketing techniques, including: success measurements of 18+ sets versus -18 sets; an in-depth review of an 18+ set; photographic research of the 18+ box art and the New York City Flagship LEGO store; and a map of LEGO’s factories and RDCs with fun facts. This site was started in 2020 when LEGO introduced the 18+ box art, featuring a plain black background with colored borders. Our interest in LEGO was generated from their shift to the adult market after marketing to children for many years. Our research pieces together to reveal which market is more successful, and where LEGO puts the most of their energy into their business, both within their stores and around the world.
